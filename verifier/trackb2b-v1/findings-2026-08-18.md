# Step 2b pre-build verification — findings (2026-08-18)

Canonical run: `canonical-run-2026-08-18.json` (harness `measure-step2b.mjs all`,
exit 0, live corpus 750 nodes / 411 edges, deterministic fcose seeds).
Criteria were fixed in `README.md` BEFORE the first run.

## T1 — Mobile reflow: PASSES with one required adjustment

Region machinery reflows cleanly at 390×844 and 360×800: region labels land
inside the viewport in every variant, zero region-label overlaps, hull compute
3–12ms. Cards need the deterministic rect-separation pass (`relaxCards`):
the depth-2 focused subgraph (20 nodes, 22 edges) comes out of fcose with 63
card overlaps under shipped params and 47–49 under card-aware params; the
separation pass clears them to ZERO in 31–166ms at this scale.

Adjustment required: at the shipped depth-2 default, fit zoom at 390px is
0.377 — cards render 60px wide, below the 72px legibility floor. Depth-1
default renders 74.5px (passes). Either mobile focuses at depth-1, or fit is
treated as orientation-only with zoom-to-read (at zoom 1, 23 visible cards,
22→0 overlaps in 46ms). Owner picks; both are honest.

## T2 — 200% text scaling: FAILS under canvas text; PASSES under DOM text

Cytoscape canvas text does not respond to browser text scaling at all. If
card text (name/date/type label) is drawn on the canvas, the 200% requirement
fails by construction — no layout parameter fixes it. This forces the
rendering decision: card text and region labels must be DOM-rendered (the
existing `graph-band-label` synced-DOM pattern already ships this technique;
`cytoscape-node-html-label` is the off-the-shelf alternative).

Under DOM cards (auto-grown at 2x: max card 320×164): focused subgraph goes
88 → 0 overlaps in 37ms after separation; desktop fit zoom 0.839, mobile
0.261 (orientation fit, zoom-to-read as above). DOM region labels at 2x: zero
overlaps, both viewports.

## T3 — Dense/expanded states: FAILS as originally framed; PASSES zoom-gated

Full-corpus all-cards is physically infeasible at 750 nodes: 505 connected
cards of 160×72 need ~4.5x the area fcose settles into; shipped params leave
27,210 card overlaps, stronger params still 16–20K (fcose local minima;
`nodeSeparation` is silently IGNORED by cytoscape-fcose 2.2.0 — verified
experimentally). Global relaxation does not converge in budget (500 iters,
5.6s, 303 residual) and balloons the bounding box. Fit zoom collapses to
0.06–0.25 — at those zooms the shipped label policy hides all text anyway
(< 0.6), so full-size cards there are pure cost.

Zoom-gated model passes everywhere a reader actually reads:
- desktop 1600×900 @ zoom 2: 19 visible, 13 → 0 overlaps, 27ms
- mobile 390×844 @ zoom 1: 23 visible, 22 → 0 overlaps, 46ms
- mobile 390×844 @ zoom 2: 5 visible, 0 overlaps, 6ms
- residual risk: max-zoom hub-centered views (zoom 3, 231 visible after prior
  spreading, 11,523 → 132 in 2.5s) — needs a focal-neighborhood-scoped
  separation at implementation, same pattern as the shipped C4 declutter.

Region boundaries at full corpus are geometrically meaningless with
type-based regions: arc-clustered layout interleaves types — every region
hull encloses ~2,000 foreign node centers and all 6 hull pairs overlap each
other. Boundaries only carry meaning inside a focused subgraph (which is
exactly what the mockup shows: one story, 4 regions, 12–14 nodes).

Secondary finding: the disconnected band's 48px pitch is calibrated to the
old 36px nodes; card-era rendering of band nodes at card zooms needs a
card-aware pitch (200px verified zero-overlap).

## Proposed adjustments held for owner ruling

1. Card text + region labels as synced DOM layers (200% scaling native);
   canvas keeps shapes, edges, boundary dashes.
2. Zoom-gated node rendering: compact shapes below 0.6 (shipped behavior
   unchanged — no text there anyway), cards at reading zooms.
3. `relaxCards` separation pass on settle + debounced pan/zoom, scoped to
   visible cards; focal-scoped variant for max-zoom hubs.
4. Region boundaries rendered in focused views only; full-corpus opt-in view
   stays compact-shape, boundary-free (per-arc boundaries at full corpus
   would be a separate decision).
5. Mobile focused default: depth-1 (legible at fit) — or depth-2 with
   fit-as-orientation. Owner picks.
